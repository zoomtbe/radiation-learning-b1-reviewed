# Radiation / In Context — B1 (review publication)

Open `full-b1/index.html` (the root page redirects there). This site is a review publication of a theoretical course module; it is not an approved training product, an examination or a certificate, and completing it does not appoint anyone to a function.

Six lessons of module B1, reviewed English scripts (version 3.6.0, 7 October 2026):

1. Getting started: radiation protection in Belgian industry — 15 chapters, 77 narrated parts, 7 interaction moments
2. The people behind radiation protection — 14 chapters, 47 narrated parts, 5 interaction moments
3. Licence, bunker and commissioning — 13 chapters, 40 narrated parts, 4 decisions
4. External workers: protection across company lines — 11 chapters, 30 narrated parts, 4 decisions
5. When something is wrong: action, information and reporting — 15 chapters, 39 narrated parts, 5 decisions
6. One Thursday: the Belgian framework in practice — 9 chapters, 20 narrated parts, 5 decisions

Playback continues through a lesson and pauses for the practice activities. Previous and next move between explanations. Speed options are 0.85×, 1× and 1.25×. Article references open a readable source panel; the Documents button opens the teaching documents of the current chapter and the official sources of the lesson. Emilia is narrated by the synthetic Microsoft voice Sonia; direct speech of other characters uses stable synthetic voices. Progress and notes are stored in the learner's browser only.

The course uses fictional scenarios and original illustrated characters. Editorial review is separate from recognised-expert approval. Sources and image credits: `full-b1/sources.html` and `full-b1/CREDITS_AND_LICENSES.txt`.
